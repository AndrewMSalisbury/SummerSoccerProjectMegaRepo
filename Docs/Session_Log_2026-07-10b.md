### Light/dark toggle

  persists in `localStorage`. Charts recolor live because all chart colors are

  page template in `se_export_writeup()` (`src/site_export.R`), not just the

### Color system (v1 was rejected — record of the feedback)

The first pass cycled many palette hues (rainbow stat tiles, five-hue grade
scale, hash-colored league tiles). **Andrew's direction: pick a few main
colors and stick to them; only the leagues should have a wide variety, one
unique color each.** Final system:

1. **Brand accents — blue + aqua only**: 3px gradient band atop the header,
   gradient mark by the site title, gradient hero text, gradient bars before
   `h2` section titles (app pages only; the writeup keeps its underline
   style), and a uniform blue top border on all stat tiles.
2. **Grades — green ↔ gray ↔ red diverging ramp** (same polarity language as
   the residual charts): A/B green (strong/soft wash), C neutral gray, D/F red
   (soft/strong). Letters stay in full-contrast ink; the hue carries only a
   background wash + border (`--grade-pos/mid/neg` + per-tier wash strength,
   `color-mix()`). Applied to leaderboard chips, team coach-history chips, and
   the big coach-page grade letter (`gradeTier()` in `components.js`). The
   "sig" and "B-team" chips stay neutral.
3. **Leagues — 14 unique fixed colors**, keyed by league slug in
   `leagueHue()` (`components.js`), replacing a name-hash that had only 8
   hues and produced duplicates. Loose identity nods where free (PL violet,
   La Liga red, Serie A blue, Ligue 1 navy, Liga Portugal green, Eredivisie
   orange, Jupiler amber, Süper Lig yellow). Used as league-tile accent bars
   on the home page; available for future league-page accents.

## Part 2: Leaderboard scope bug (reported by Andrew)

**Symptom:** José Ramón Sandoval's leaderboard row showed 4 stints / 77 games
/ 2 clubs; his coach page showed 13 / 278 / 5.

**Cause:** the leaderboard passed `n_stints`/`total_games`/`n_clubs` straight
from `coach_grades_top5.rds`, which counts **top-5-league stints only**, while
coach pages compute career totals from the all-league stint table. Coaches
whose whole career is top-5 (Guardiola) coincide, hiding the mismatch.

**Fix:** `se_export_small()` now computes full-career totals from `d$cr`
(same definition as the coach pages) and uses those on the leaderboard;
grade/BLUP/rank/mean-residual stay cut-scoped and cut-labeled (each matches a
number visible on the coach page). The leaderboard subtitle now states that
stints/clubs/games are full-career totals across all 14 leagues.

## Part 3: Stint-duplication bug in M5 (found while verifying Part 2)

### Discovery

After the leaderboard fix, Marcel Rapp's counts moved the *wrong way* (grades
said 3 stints / 42 games; career computed as 2 / 38). Career can never be
smaller than one cut — something upstream was inflated.

### Mechanism (verified in data, then in code)

In `build_coach_residuals()` (`coach_attribution.R`), match attribution
already totals a coach's full-season games once — but the subsequent
`left_join(coach_dates)` joined **one row per tenure bracket**. A coach with
two brackets in the same team-season (caretaker then permanent, or sacked and
re-appointed — e.g. Rapp's 4-game Hoffenheim 2019/20 spell, Tom Cleverley's
9-game Watford 2023/24 spell) got their identical full-stint row emitted
twice. **79 (team-season, coach) pairs were duplicated**, every one with
identical games/points/residual per bracket — double-weighting those stints in
`compute_coach_stats`, the significance tests, and the mixed model/BLUPs.

The website exporter and M6 (`cf_build_analysis_table`) had already deduped
these rows independently (July 9–10), which is why coach pages were right and
M6 was never affected.

### Fix

`build_coach_residuals()` now collapses `coach_dates` to one row per coach
before the join (`arrange(date_from) |> distinct(coach_id, .keep_all = TRUE)`
— earliest bracket, the same rule the downstream dedupes used). The downstream
dedupes in `site_export.R` and `coach_fit.R` are kept as no-op guards with
updated comments.

### M5 re-run (both cuts, same thresholds as the 06-17 run)

Inputs (`residuals_top5.rds`, `residuals_14league.rds`) are team-season
residuals, unaffected by the bug — only M5 outputs were rebuilt:
`coach_residuals_*`, `coach_ranked_*`, `coach_blups_*`, then
`save_coach_grades()`.

| | old | new |
|---|---|---|
| 14-league stints | 8,286 | 8,207 (−79; 0 duplicate pairs remain) |
| Graded, top-5 cut | 338 | 334 |
| Graded, 14-league cut | 975 | 959 |
| Significant after FDR (both cuts) | 3 (Ferguson, Guardiola, Xavi) | 3 (same) |
| 14-league variance: coach / club | 1.9% / 3.4%, LRT p = 0.0088 | 1.8% / 3.2%, χ² = 6.01, p = 0.0142 |
| Top-5 variance: coach / club | 3.6% / 3.6%, LRT p = 0.0072 | 3.5% / 3.9%, χ² = 6.80, p = 0.0091 |
| Guardiola BLUP (top-5) | +0.118, rank 1 | +0.114, rank 1 |

BLUPs correlate 0.999 (top-5) / 0.998 (14-league) with the old run; the top-5
top 10 is unchanged in order. The graded-coach drop is coaches falling below
the ≥3-stints bar once their phantom stint vanished — notably **Marcel Rapp
(was rank 7, A+, off a double-counted 4-game hot streak) and Tom Cleverley are
now correctly unranked**. Eddie Howe replaces Jorge Jesus at #15.
