# Session Log — 2026-07-14 (Session 2): Team Builder

## Purpose

Record of decisions, code changes, and verification. This session designed and
implemented the **team builder**: a new site page where the user assembles a
custom XI on a drawn pitch (any of the 22 observed formations, click-a-circle
player picking from the full big-5 pool) and gets coach suggestions for the
team they built, using the same similarity engine as the team pages — computed
client-side against the user's XI. Design: `Docs/Team_Builder_Design.md`
(agreed at session start via four settled questions, then one-shot built).

---

## Settled design decisions (Andrew)

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Player pool | All big-5, any season (2015/16–2024/25) — cross-era XIs allowed |
| Coach suggestion | Similarity grid (team-page engine: cosine on archetype shares, 85/15 quality blend) |
| Player photos | New TM headshot scrape (coach-image pattern), initials fallback |
| Position eligibility | The recommender's `cr_archetype_slot_matrix` (+ TM-position fallback) |

## Phase 0 — pool sizing (all better than the design's estimates)

- **5,570 unique TM players** (estimate was 8–10k): 17,132 archetype
  player-seasons matched to TM identity (99.5% of 17,219; 87 lost to the known
  youth-player crosswalk residue) + 1,506 GK seasons (442 GKs, ≥600 min).
- `players.json` measures **2.4 MB** → single file, no position-group split.
- Photos average ~6 KB → **~34 MB projected, committed to the repo** (in line
  with the ~39 MB of coach images already committed).

## Phase 1 — player photo scraper (`source_data.r`)

`xx_raw_player_image_url()` + `xx_data_populate_player_images()`, cloning the
coach-image pattern but with the **nationality scraper's NULL/NA semantics**
(the coach-image scraper records transient fetch failures as permanently
missing — a known flaw, not propagated): fetch failure → retried next run with
backoff (120 s, abort after 5 consecutive); page-loaded-but-placeholder →
recorded NA (the `default` portrait is detected and skipped). Files land at
`data/images/players/<tm_id>.<ext>`, lookup `data/cache/player_images.rds`,
priority order = career big-5 minutes (Lewandowski/Salah/Kane first) so an
interrupted run covers the players people search for. Run launched at 2.5 s
pacing (slightly gentler than the standard 2 s while the nationality scrape
may still be sharing the WAF cookie); **still running at session close**
(~400+ done, zero failures). The exporter picks up whatever coverage exists.

## Phase 2 — exports

- **`cr_save_results()`** (`coach_recommender.R`) now persists a `builder`
  component into `recommender.rds`: the similarity-pool profiles (81 coaches,
  ≥4 big-5 stints, positive career residual — raw 11-dim share vectors, which
  the browser needs to score arbitrary XIs), plus `cr_formation_slots`, the
  archetype→slot and TM-position→slot eligibility matrices, and
  `pa_archetype_labels`. Regenerated by re-running `cr_build_scorer()` and
  reusing the saved payoff folds (the pre-registered validation is carried
  forward, not re-run). Side benefit: the refreshed career facts picked up the
  much fuller nationality cache (3,216 coaches).
- **`se_export_builder()`** (`site_export.R`) writes `site/data/builder/`:
  `players.json` (pool grouped by TM id: name, photo, per-season league/club/
  crest-id/value/TM-position/archetype), `coaches.json` (profiles + grade/BLUP
  + badge facts), `meta.json` (22 formations **with hand-mapped pitch
  coordinates** in `se_formation_layouts` — hard-stop validated against the
  model's slot counts at export — eligibility matrices, archetype labels,
  thresholds, and per-league distributions of latest-season best-XI values for
  the level percentile). Player photos copy to `site/assets/players/`.

## Phases 3–5 — frontend (`site/builder.html`, `site/js/builder.js`, CSS)

- Vertical SVG pitch (own goal at the bottom), formation `<select>` grouped by
  family; switching formations **keeps picks** via greedy highest-eligibility
  re-assignment; displaced players land on a bench strip (click = auto-place
  into the best empty eligible slot, × = remove).
- Slot click → picker modal: rows grouped **Natural (1.0) / Capable (≥0.5) /
  Stretch (≥0.25)** by the eligibility matrix, value-sorted, side-matching
  players first for sided slots, 60 rows per tier (search lifts the cap), one
  row per player with an inline season `<select>` (newest first). The same
  player can appear only once per XI (other seasons disable with a note).
  Filled circles show the photo (initials fallback until the scrape reaches a
  player) + name/season label. Surname particles are preserved ("De Bruyne").
- Team readout: XI value, fill count, value-level percentile vs the exported
  best-XI distributions, an archetype-mix bar. A note flags cross-era value
  mixing. **No predicted points for the XI** — a fantasy team is outside the
  M3 model's support (design §5).
- Suggestions at 11/11: equal-weighted outfield archetype shares → cosine
  similarity vs the 81 profiles → 85/15 z-blend → the same `.sim-grid` card
  layout as team pages, ranks assigned once over the unfiltered pool. Filter
  chips as on team pages, plus a **league-context selector** (a custom team
  has no league): without it the league/country/domestic chips disable with a
  tooltip. Footnote carries the descriptive-similarity labeling verbatim.
- URL-hash persistence: `#f=<formation>&s=<pid.year,…>` — bookmarkable,
  restores on fresh load (invalid/duplicate/ineligible tokens dropped safely).
- "Team builder" added to the shared header nav (`components.js`).
- New CSS: `--pitch`/`--pitch-line` variables added to all three theme blocks
  (both dark copies kept identical), plus builder/picker/modal/bench styles.

## Bugs found and fixed during QA

1. **Picker tier starvation:** a global 150-row cap let a huge Natural group
   (964 for ST) hide Capable/Stretch entirely → per-tier 60-row cap.
2. **"Kevin De Bruyne" → "Bruyne"** in slot labels → particle-aware surname
   shortening (de/van/di/…).
3. Two QA-script artifacts (not app bugs), recorded for future chromote work:
   navigating `page#hash` from `page` is a fragment jump (no reload — test
   restores via `about:blank` first), and top-level `const` in
   `Runtime.evaluate` persists in the page's global scope across calls (wrap
   every snippet in an IIFE).

## Post-review revisions (same day, after Andrew's feedback)

Four changes requested on first use; three settled via questions (Stretch =
search-only; list value = career peak; formation UI = button grid):

1. **Picker regrouped from tiers to one value-sorted list.** The old
   Natural/Capable/Stretch grouping made each slot read as a single archetype's
   list. Now: one row per player, Natural + Capable together, sorted by
   career-peak value; "Capable" tag where the best fit is < 1.0; Stretch-only
   players reachable via search under an "Out of position" header; 80-row cap.
2. **Season moved to a popup**: clicking a row opens a season chooser (club,
   archetype, per-season value, Stretch tags; back button preserves the search
   query); single-season players place immediately. The inline season
   `<select>` is gone.
3. **Formation selector is now a chip grid** in the right column below the
   team summary (grouped back-4 / back-3-or-5, current highlighted); the
   top control bar was removed and "Clear team" moved into the summary header.
4. **Photo crop fix**: TM headshots are 139×181 portrait, so `object-fit:
   cover` in a square circle unavoidably crops 30% of the height (insetting
   the image only shrinks the circle, not the crop — first attempt failed
   exactly that way). Fixed with `object-fit: contain`: the whole photo scales
   into the circle with small side gaps; applied to slot, picker, and bench
   photos. Verified in both themes.
5. **Back-3/back-5 middle CB no longer covers the GK** (Andrew's catch): the
   old layouts put the middle CB at (50, 13) with the GK at (50, 5) — the
   circles genuinely overlapped. `se_formation_layouts` now uses a flat back
   three/five at y = 20, with knock-on moves in the three formations that
   stack a DM/CM directly above the middle CB (3-1-4-2 DM 28→34 + CMs
   widened to 36/64; 3-3-1-3 CM mid 34→35; 3-3-3-1 DM 34→35). The name
   labels also moved **onto the circle bottom** (was hanging below), because
   in any vertical stack a below-the-circle chip lands on the face beneath —
   the back-3 CB label would have covered the GK's face even with the circles
   apart. A pairwise ellipse-overlap validator over all 22 exported layouts
   now reports zero overlapping circle pairs (tightest: 3-1-4-2 DM~CB at
   metric 1.03); 3-5-2 / 5-3-2 / 4-3-3 screenshot-verified. Re-export also
   picked up 1,151 player photos (scrape ongoing).
6. **On-circle labels slimmed** (the two-line chip from #5 crept up over
   chins): the pitch label is now a single name-only line at the circle's
   bottom edge, and the season moved to a small "24/25" pill at the circle's
   top-left — which is photo *background* under `object-fit: contain`, so it
   never covers a face. Full name + season remain in the hover title.
   Single-line chips also stay narrow enough not to collide between
   same-row neighbours (e.g. 4-4-2's central midfield pair).
7. **Name chips lowered further** (Andrew: still too high on the photo): the
   chip now hangs mostly below the circle (`bottom: -10px`), and the layouts
   moved to guarantee ~10px of air beneath every circle — GK y 5→2 (deeper
   in his box, chip still inside the pitch border), back-3/back-5 lines
   20→18 (level with the back-4 CBs), and 3-1-4-2's CM pair widened to
   30/70 so its circle no longer covered the DM's season pill. Overlap
   validator still zero pairs (tightest now 5-3-2 CB~CB, metric 1.17);
   4-3-3 / 3-5-2 / 5-3-2 / 3-1-4-2 screenshot-verified.

Verified by a fourth chromote round (`qa_builder4.R`): ST list = Mbappé €200m
→ Haaland → Messi/Neymar/Vinicius (Capable-tagged) by value; Van Dijk still
absent at ST; Salah season chooser shows all 10 seasons with per-season values
